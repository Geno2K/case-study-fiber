## **Assumptions:** In order to anonymize and fictionalize the data, the dataset uses market\_1, market\_2, and market\_3 to indicate three different city service areas the data represents. The data also lists five problem types:

* ## Type\_1 is account management

* ## Type\_2 is technician troubleshooting

* ## Type\_4 is construction

* ## Type\_5 is internet and wifi

## Additionally, the dataset also records repeat calls over seven day periods. The initial contact date is listed as contacts\_n. The other call columns are then contacts\_n\_number of days since first call. For example, contacts\_n\_6 indicates six days since first contact.
